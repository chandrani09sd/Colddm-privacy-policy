1. Introduction

Welcome to Colddm.

Colddm is a career assistance application designed to help users discover job opportunities, identify relevant professional contacts, and prepare personalized professional outreach messages.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information processed through our application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is shared, and the choices available to users.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, Colddm may process the following information:

Account Information

  • Name and email address.
  • Account authentication information.
  • User preferences and settings.

Job Search Information

  • Target company names.
  • Job titles and job descriptions.
  • Saved job listings.
  • Job application preferences.

Professional Information

  • Professional names and designations.
  • Publicly available professional profile information.
  • LinkedIn profile URLs.
  • Company and employment information.

User-Provided Information

  • Information entered manually by users.
  • Professional messages and outreach drafts.
  • Resume or career-related information, if uploaded.

Technical Information

  • Device and browser information.
  • Application usage information.
  • Error logs and diagnostic information.

We only process information necessary to provide the application’s functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Colddm may be used to:

  • Discover relevant job opportunities.
  • Organize saved jobs and target companies.
  • Identify relevant professional contacts.
  • Help users identify hiring managers, recruiters and technical professionals.
  • Generate personalized cold outreach messages.
  • Improve search relevance and application functionality.
  • Maintain application security and prevent misuse.

4. LinkedIn Integration

Colddm may use LinkedIn-related integrations and authorized third-party data providers to support professional discovery and profile identification.

When LinkedIn-related information is processed:

  • Information is used only for the application’s intended professional discovery features.
  • LinkedIn profile URLs are used to direct users to relevant professional profiles.
  • LinkedIn information is handled according to applicable LinkedIn terms and permissions.
  • Colddm does not claim affiliation with or endorsement by LinkedIn.

Colddm does not guarantee access to every LinkedIn profile.

5. AI and Third-Party Services

Colddm may use third-party services for:

  • AI-powered message generation.
  • Job discovery and search.
  • Professional contact discovery.
  • Application hosting and infrastructure.
  • Authentication and data storage.

These services may process information necessary to provide their respective functionality.

Such processing is subject to the applicable providers’ privacy policies and contractual requirements.

6. Information Sharing

Colddm does not sell users’ personal information.

Information may be shared with authorized service providers when necessary to operate the application, provide requested functionality, maintain security or comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not authorize third-party service providers to use information beyond their permitted service purposes.

7. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through Colddm.

These measures may include:

  • Access controls.
  • Secure authentication.
  • Protected API credentials.
  • Secure data transmission.
  • Appropriate storage and retention practices.

However, no internet-based application can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application’s services or meet applicable legal obligations.

Users may request deletion of their account information and associated personal data by contacting us.

Certain information may need to be retained where legally required or necessary for legitimate security purposes.

9. User Rights and Choices

Users may:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Stop using the application.
  • Revoke applicable third-party authorizations.

Requests can be submitted using the contact information below.
